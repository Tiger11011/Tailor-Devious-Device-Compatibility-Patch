# Changelog / 更新日志

## 0.6.14 — 2026-10-21

### English

- Added previews of how characters stand or move while wearing supported restraint gear.
- Fixed arms staying in the previous restrained position after removing the gear and changing clothes.
- Fixed characters unexpectedly standing up when they should remain crawling in the fitting room.
- Fixed earlier outfit selections reappearing during repeated changes, and improved removal of leftover clothing.
- Improved body-colour recovery after loading a save for characters whose appearance was changed with Mu Dynamic NPC Replacer.
- Fixed crashes that could occur during certain outfit changes.

### 中文

- 新增试衣时受支持拘束装备对应的站姿或动作展示。
- 修复脱下拘束装备并换衣后，手臂仍保持原来拘束姿势的问题。
- 修复试衣时本应保持爬行，却自动站起来的问题。
- 修复连续换装时之前的衣服重新出现的情况，并改善上一套服装残留的问题。
- 改善使用 Mu Dynamic NPC Replacer 更换外观的角色在读档后的身体肤色恢复。
- 修复部分换装操作可能导致的崩溃。

Installation and requirements / 安装与前置要求：[English](INSTALLATION-English.md) · [中文](INSTALLATION.zh-CN.md)
